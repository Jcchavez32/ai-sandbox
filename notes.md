# Quick quotes
- Millwork shop owners spend a lot of time preparing quotes. 
- Creating a database with specifications, quantities and costs involved in each item. 
- The tool will produce a quote that includes not just the cost of the items, but takes overhead into account by adding itemized costs that the estimator must confirm.
- The tool reminds the user monthly to confirm prices are up to date, since some costs are not searchable to AI agent.
- Certain costs will be available online, AI agent must check these daily and alert the user if they increase by more than 5%.
